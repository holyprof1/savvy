Our Chief
Storyteller

Tofunmi Akinseye is a visionary magazine publisher and PR specialist with a relentless passion for storytelling and amplifying Africa's voice on the global stage. As Founder and Publisher of Savvy Magazine, she has curated 17 impactful editions and led high-impact PR campaigns for Black Panther, Avatar, MTN, Filmhouse and other leading brands. Through training partnerships with Google and Facebook, she has empowered over 500 young Nigerians, nurturing Africa's next generation of storytellers and leaders. Her excellence has been recognised by UN MIPAD (Class of 2024, Media & Culture), Africa Under 40 CEOs, PR Personality of the Year and multiple awards positioning Savvy Media Africa as a young, culture-moving agency.
